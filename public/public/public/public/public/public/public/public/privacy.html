EatHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy describes how EatHub plans to handle information when its account, restaurant and menu features become operational.

1. Information collected

Depending on the features used, information may include restaurant names, contact details, menu items, prices, account identifiers and service activity.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to display menus, manage restaurant accounts, maintain security and operate platform features.

3. Access and security

The platform is intended to separate each restaurant's private information and restrict account functions to authorized users. These protections must be implemented and tested before launch.

4. Sharing information

Public menu information may be visible to customers. Private account information should not be exposed publicly. Any third-party services used must be disclosed in the final policy.

5. Retention and deletion

Retention periods and account-deletion procedures must be defined before public launch. Users should be provided with a contact channel for privacy requests.

6. Your privacy rights

Privacy rights depend on applicable law. Before launch, the operator must review relevant Kenyan data-protection requirements and publish accurate contact details.

7. Policy updates

This draft must be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual data collected, storage arrangements and operational practices.
